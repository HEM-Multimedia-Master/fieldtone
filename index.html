FIELDTONE

Hold a pad or keys 1–6 to play. Release to fade out. Multiple fingers play independent voices.

Sound sets

R generates a set. ← / Space goes back; → / Shift+Space goes forward. History lasts until reload; generating from an older set replaces its future.

Envelope

Drag A, D, S, R horizontally to move time boundaries (0.005–5 seconds per stage). Neighbouring durations change together. S is a minimum sustain duration at 60% of peak; holding extends it.

Drag A or R vertically: up = logarithmic, middle = linear, down = power. Tab to a point: ← / → adjusts time (Shift for larger steps); ↑ / ↓ on A or R changes its curve.

Filter gesture

Hold a pad and drag across the full window. Vertical: cutoff frequency, 40 Hz at the bottom to 16 kHz at the top. Horizontal: Q, 0.2 at the left to 18 at the right. Each pad keeps its settings in its sound set.

↑ / ↓ changes volume in 20% steps with a 500 ms fade (default 80%). Envelope points use their own arrow controls when focused.

I opens this help; Esc closes it. Opening help or leaving the page releases held sounds.
